Questions families ask

Is Alicia a real person?

No. Alicia is an artificial-intelligence voice assistant, and she always says so — no tricks. She’s designed to be warm, patient and respectful, and to sound like someone from home.

Do my parents need to install anything?

No. No apps, no internet, no smartphone setup. Alicia calls their mobile phone at the time you choose — they just answer.

What number does Alicia call from?

From +1 (863) 344-5774 in Colombia and Latin America, and +1 (561) 464-4320 in the United States. Ask them to save it as “Alicia” so they recognize her.

What does Alicia talk about?

How they slept and how they feel, their memories, music, cooking, family, their neighborhood — whatever they enjoy. She remembers the last conversation and never pushes topics like politics or religion.

What do I receive?

A short text after each call, a notice if they didn’t answer, and an immediate alert if they mention a fall, strong pain or something worrying. In My account you can see every summary, change the call time and pause calls.

Is Alicia an emergency service?

No. Alicia keeps them company and lets you know if something seems off, but she doesn’t replace doctors or emergency services. In an emergency, call 123 in Colombia or 911 in the US.

How much does it cost and how do I pay?

Plans start at US$19.99 a month with a 7-day free trial. You pay securely through Mercado Pago (cards and Nequi), in Colombian pesos. Cancel anytime from My account. See our Refund policy.

Can my mom or dad call Alicia?

Not yet — for now Alicia calls at the scheduled time. Calling Alicia anytime is coming soon.

What happens with their information?

We use it only to make the calls and update the family. We never sell it or use it for ads, and you can delete everything anytime. Read our Privacy policy.
